Superego Software LTD is a one-person specialist studio with over a decade of professional experience in the video game industry. I focus on the technical side of game development: the code, systems, and tooling that keeps games running at their best across any platform.

From entire game porting (my passion :) ) to gameplay features and custom made developer tools, I partner with studios of all sizes to deliver clean, maintainable, high-performance code.

I’ve worked on games on most of the modern platforms PC, PS4, PS5, Xbox One, XSX, XSS, WinGDK, Switch, Switch 2, and more.
